For biaxial KTP, changing either θ or φ generally changes these two indices.
Biaxial calculation: for each k̂(θ,φ), the optical impermeability tensor diag(1/nₓ², 1/nᵧ², 1/n_z²) is projected onto the plane perpendicular to k̂. The two eigenvalues ρ₁,ρ₂ of that 2×2 transverse matrix give n₁=1/√ρ₁ and n₂=1/√ρ₂.

Display convention: eigenvector signs are chosen continuously between slider positions. Since e and −e describe the same polarization axis, this removes nonphysical 180° arrow flips without changing the calculated indices.
