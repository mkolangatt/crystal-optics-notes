Change φ while holding θ fixed: the plane and k rotate around z, but both refractive-index values remain unchanged.
Uniaxial result: no is constant and 1 / neff²(θ) = cos²θ / no² + sin²θ / ne². There is no φ term because nx = ny.
Reference for refractive-index model:
Gayer, O., Sacks, Z., Galun, E., & Arie, A. (2008). Temperature and wavelength dependent refractive index equations for MgO-doped congruent and stoichiometric LiNbO₃. Applied Physics B, 91, 343–348. DOI: 10.1007/s00340-008-2998-2
