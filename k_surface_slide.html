θ, φ convention

θ (polar angle): measured down from the z-axis to the propagation direction k.

φ (azimuthal angle): measured around the z-axis, within the xy-plane, from the x-axis to the projection of k.

Direction components: a = sinθcosφ, b = sinθsinφ, c = cosθ.
