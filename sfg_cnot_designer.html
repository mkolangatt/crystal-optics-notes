Gate summary

100% = a perfectly phase-matched (y = 1) 50 mm MgO:LN crystal using d33 = 25 pm/V, with the same pump, for both materials; d33 in the equations is this reference. The crosstalk uses the same scale.

Model and assumptions

  • Refractive indices: 5 mol% MgO:LN, Gayer et al., Appl. Phys. B 91, 343 (2008); H = z (extraordinary), V = y (ordinary), propagation along x. Wavevector k(ν)=2π n(λ,T)λ, λ=cν\displaystyle k(\nu) = \dfrac{2\pi\, n(\lambda,T)}{\lambda},\ \lambda = \dfrac{c}{\nu}.
  • KTP (two-crystal mode only): Sellmeier and thermo-optic dispersion of Kato & Takaoka, Appl. Opt. 41, 5040 (2002) (dn/dT fitted over 0.43–1.58 µm); H along z uses nz, the other polarization ny; d33 = 14.6 pm/V (Shoji et al., JOSA B 14, 2268 (1997)). KTP has no d22 (point group mm2), so a single-crystal type-0 design is not possible.
  • Poling periods are the values at the operating temperature, Λ = 2π/K. Conversion to the fabricated (room-temperature) period is left to the vendor.
  • One crystal: cascaded grating, the first wHL poled with ΛH and the rest with ΛV (wV = V share).
  • Weak conversion: pumps undepleted and each photon only slightly converted, so η ∝ deff²·L²·y with y the phase-matching factor of the actual domain pattern (first-order limit of η = sin²(κL), P. Kumar, Opt. Lett. 15, 1476 (1990); first-order QPM coupling (2/π)deff, M. M. Fejer et al., IEEE J. Quantum Electron. 28, 2631 (1992)). Same pump power for every design, equal V-pump strengths, plane waves, no loss.
  • 100% = a perfectly phase-matched (y = 1) 50 mm MgO:LN crystal (d33 = 25 pm/V) with the same pump, so η = (d/d33,LN)²·(L/50 mm)²·y for both materials. The reference does not depend on spacing, length, temperature, configuration or material, so all designs share one scale.
  • Each V photon couples to its target output (through the right V pump) and to its crosstalk output (through the other V pump).
  • Mixed-polarization (type-I and type-II) processes through d31 and d15 are excluded for now; they lie far from every grating feature.
  • Two-crystal configuration: the H crystal (H along z) is followed by the V crystal rotated 90° (V along z). Each has a single period; all pumps pass through both. Processes of the other polarization in each crystal (through d22) are excluded; they are far from phase-matching.
  • H–V delay: each photon converts uniformly along its crystal or section and travels at its group velocity, β = dk/dω = ng/c (o = ordinary, e = extraordinary); free-space gaps are not included.
  • Not modelled yet: absolute pump power, strong conversion and back-conversion, Raman noise from the pumps, pump polarization extinction, crystal rotation misalignment.
