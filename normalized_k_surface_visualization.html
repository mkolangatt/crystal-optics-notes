θ, φ convention

θ (polar angle): measured down from the z-axis to the propagation direction k.

φ (azimuthal angle): measured around the z-axis, within the xy-plane, from the x-axis to the projection of k.

Direction cosines: a = sinθcosφ, b = sinθsinφ, c = cosθ.

What is being plotted?

Each surface is a constant-frequency wave-vector surface, normalized by ω/c: k/(ω/c) = n k̂. The radial distance therefore equals the allowed refractive index for that propagation direction.

This is not the index ellipsoid. The index ellipsoid is the geometric construction used to obtain the two allowed refractive indices for a chosen direction; this plot collects those allowed indices over all directions to build the two complete wave-vector sheets.
